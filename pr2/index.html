WELCOME TO THE GRAND ORDER

A little order.
A small miracle.

Organize numbered souls into Choirs and Processions, invoke your Angel’s gift, and empty your hand before your rivals. Each round brings a new decree. After three rounds, the highest score becomes God’s Favorite.
