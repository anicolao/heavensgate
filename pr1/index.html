WELCOME TO THE GRAND ORDER

A little order.
A small miracle.

Organize numbered souls into Choirs and Processions, invoke your Angel’s gift, and empty your hand before your rivals. Each round brings a new decree. After three rounds, the highest score becomes God’s Favorite.

Bring your own Rummikub tiles and racks. We’ll provide the heavenly paperwork.

ANGEL

Zadkiel

Forgiveness

During Organize

Take one unprotected soul from Heaven into your hand. Finish Heaven legally. Retrieval costs no Favor; playing that soul in the same action or later earns Favor normally.

HEAVENLY DECREE

The Eternal Circle

Rounds one & two

Same-color Processions may wrap 13 → 1. Minimum three, maximum thirteen souls; no repeated rank within one Procession. Choirs are unchanged.

REPLACES AFFECTED RULESHG · DECREE-5
FINAL JUDGMENT

The Scales of Justice

Round three

Set aside up to five numbered hand souls on your rack. Delivery earns an immediate, permanent printed-rank credit; the tile then becomes ordinary. Undelivered commitments cost triple. Transfers outside Heaven cancel commitment; Communion delivery credits the owner. No jokers.
