THE HEAVENLY PRINT SHOP

Bring Heaven to the table.

You already have the souls. Here is the paperwork.

Use your own standard 106-tile Rummikub set and racks. Print 27 cards plus table essentials; bring an opaque bag, five coins, and pencils.

Adjust double-sided alignment

Print pages 1–2 first. Hold them to the light. Directions refer to the back as you look at it. If marks are too far right, use a negative horizontal offset; too low, use a negative vertical offset.

Print at actual size

  1. Choose the same paper size here and in your print dialog. Use portrait, 100% / actual size, no browser headers or footers.
  2. Enable background graphics for full color. Low ink removes painted layers and uses black type on white.
  3. Cards are 63 × 88 mm, nine per sheet. For both sides, use long-edge duplex; pages alternate fronts and mirrored backs. Test the first pair before printing all six pages.
  4. Cut along the crop marks. Sleeve with spare backing cards if your paper is thin. Matching category backs conceal card identities.

On US Letter, the nine-card grid leaves 7.7 mm top/bottom margins. Do not use “fit to page.” Small printer registration errors are expected; test before cutting.

Forgiveness

During Organize

Penance

During Organize

Move one unprotected soul from Heaven to your Purgatory while arranging and playing hand souls. Later, while occupied, rescue it into a Procession or draw. Rescue permits hand play immediately. Purgatory never prevents going out. No Favor is lost on removal or earned on rescue.

Before your action

Calling

Before your action

Name a rank, 1–13. Every opponent gives you all hand souls of that rank, including rack commitments. Jokers have no rank. Resolve all transfers, check for players going out, then take your ordinary action if the round continues.

Before your action

Choose a rank in your hand and one opponent. Give them all your hand souls of that rank. Jokers cannot be included. An empty hand ends the round; otherwise take your ordinary action.

FINAL JUDGMENT

Divine Retribution

Round three

All tied score leaders after round two are God’s Favorites. If a non-Favorite wins, double every Favorite’s penalty, increasing the winner’s award. If a Favorite wins, score normally. In stalemate, choose the winner before doubling.

FINAL JUDGMENT

Shared Sins

Round three

After the deal, secretly choose another player. At round end, reveal and gain a credit equal to their penalty loss before credits. Selecting the winner earns zero. In stalemate, copy their penalty-difference loss. Your credit changes only your score.

ROUND THREE ONLYHG · JUDGMENT-2
FINAL JUDGMENT

The Scales of Justice

Round three

Set aside up to five numbered hand souls on your rack. Delivery earns an immediate, permanent printed-rank credit; the tile then becomes ordinary. Undelivered commitments cost triple. Transfers outside Heaven cancel commitment; Communion delivery credits the owner. No jokers.

ROUND THREE ONLYHG · JUDGMENT-3
FINAL JUDGMENT

Divine Favor Head Start

Round three

Start with Favor equal to the number of players with a strictly higher cumulative score after round two. Tied players get the same head start. Continue earning Favor normally.

ROUND THREE ONLYHG · JUDGMENT-4
FINAL JUDGMENT

Redemption

Round three

The round winner replaces their worst negative score from rounds one or two with zero. If equal, erase round one. If neither is negative, gain no benefit. Other players’ scores are unchanged.
